The Program

The 10-Week Mass Blueprint

Engineered for extreme muscle growth over 10 weeks, this routine brutally targets each muscle group once a week through heavy, multi-joint lifts. You'll hit the iron four days a week, keeping Wednesdays and your weekends completely open for recovery. But remember: training is only half the battle. To see real size, you must consume a massive amount of food—think huge, frequent meals at a strict caloric surplus to force your body to grow.

🏋️

Core Compound Movements

Maximize your muscular development and power output by prioritizing the bench press, squat, deadlift, and military press.

📅

Optimized Training Schedule

A structured 4-day split (Monday/Tuesday and Thursday/Friday), ensuring you get essential downtime on Wednesdays and over the weekend.

🍽️

Hypertrophy Nutrition

Support your gains with a high-calorie diet, consuming five or more large meals every day to facilitate recovery.

📈

Strategic Progression

Continually challenge your muscles by systematically increasing the weight load as the number of repetitions decreases throughout your sets.
